The Story

✉️
Myeongnan Brand Laboratoryfrom. Director Kim Cho-ryang

During renovation work at our lab, the workers found an old parcel. It seemed too meaningful to simply throw away — but no one knows who it belongs to, or what it even is.

Detective, the clues are hidden all around Busan Station. Crack the codes, follow the trail, and reveal the true identity of the lost parcel.
